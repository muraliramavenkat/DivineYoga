First time here?

  1. Extract the entire ZIP. On Windows, right-click it and choose Extract All. On Mac, double-click the ZIP.
  2. Open START_HERE.html from the extracted folder in your browser.
  3. Select Choose a daily routine, or browse by yoga style or body area.
  4. Read each guide, choose a comfortable variation, and adjust the routine to suit you.

Keep the complete folder together. Images and instructions are included, so an internet connection, account or installation is not required.

A routine at your pace

Choose a gentle Hatha foundation, quiet Yin, easy Vinyasa or steady Power routine. Add poses, reorder or remove steps, and shorten timers. Print your routine to keep a copy. Changes apply to the currently open page.

Timers stop at the end of each step. Release slowly, take a rest, then select Next when ready. You can pause or skip at any time.

Sharing with a friend

Send the complete DivineYoga-Share.zip as a file, or use a file-sharing link. Your friend should download and extract it before opening this page. Sending just an HTML file will leave out its images and guides.

This edition is designed for Windows and Mac computers. Opening a multi-file offline website on phones and tablets varies by device; use a computer for this package.

Educational practice guide. Use the modifications and precautions, and seek qualified guidance for unfamiliar positions or health conditions. A body-area tag does not establish suitability for pain or injury. External reference links need internet, but the guide itself works offline.
