## Naming and image notes

- **Butterfly:** Yin Butterfly is a practice variation, not an exact synonym for the active Bound Angle pose. Supported upright Butterfly; the optional forward fold is not shown.
- **Sphinx:** Common modern Sanskrit name; Yin teaching also uses the English name. 
- **Low Dragon:** A supported Yin lunge; keep it distinct from the more active Low Lunge guide. Supported on blocks with the rear knee padded.
- **Reclined Figure Four:** Also called Eye of the Needle or Reclined Pigeon; naming varies by school. Bottom foot stays on the floor; drawing the thigh closer is optional.
- **Reclined Twist:** The guide shows a generic bent-knee supine twist. Its exact traditional Sanskrit identity is not established, so no specific name is guessed. Supported bent-knee variation with a neutral gaze.
- **Corpse Pose / Savasana:** Relaxation is the main action; no stretching or strengthening claim. 
- **Cat–Cow:** Two complementary postures are retained as the existing combined guide. Cat on the left; Cow on the right. Knees and wrists are weight bearing.
- **Downward-Facing Dog:** Shoulder involvement does not mean a neck stretch. Keep knees bent as needed; heels need not reach the mat.
- **Low Lunge:** The current guide uses hands on the thigh or blocks, rather than an overhead-arm backbend. 
- **Warrior II:** The shoulder tag reflects sustained arm support, not neck stretching. 
- **Child’s Pose:** Deep knee flexion is a load to assess, not a reason to list this as a knee treatment. Bolster-supported variation with forward arms.
- **Chair:** The shoulder category refers to arm support, not neck stretching. Arms-forward variation.
- **Plank:** Difficulty describes the standard form; the preserved image shows a lighter knee-down version. Knee-down Plank, not full toe-supported Plank.
- **Supported Side Plank:** This is a knee-supported variation, not the full stacked-foot Side Plank. Lower knee supported and upper hand on hip.
- **Bridge:** The lower back participates but is not the intended strengthening target. 
- **Tree:** Support and foot height alter the balance demand. Chair-supported calf variation; beginner instructions also offer toes on the floor.
- **Triangle:** The image is the supported extended-triangle shape. High-block supported variation; keep the front knee soft.
- **Supine Banana Stretch:** This is a modern Yin name rather than a classical Sanskrit identity. 
- **Supported Caterpillar:** Yin Caterpillar is related to a seated forward fold, not an exact equivalent of its active version. 
- **Supported Puppy / Melting Heart:** Puppy and Yin Melting Heart names overlap across schools; this guide is the supported knees-down shape. 
- **Thread the Needle:** This is the kneeling shoulder-thread variation, distinct from supine Figure Four. 
- **Neutral Tabletop:** Modern Sanskrit usage varies; this is neutral hands-and-knees, not Reverse Tabletop. 
- **Bird Dog:** Commonly treated as a modern balance drill; no exact traditional identity is assumed. Opposite arm and leg lifted; lighter options keep a limb on the floor.
- **Low Cobra:** The image and instructions show low Cobra, not Upward-Facing Dog. 
- **Supported Standing Forward Fold:** This is a chair-supported partial version, not the deepest form of Uttanasana. 
- **Dolphin:** This is Dolphin with both forearms down, not a forearm balance. 
- **Forearm Plank:** No single exact Sanskrit name is assigned across schools. Knee-down Forearm Plank is pictured.
- **Reverse Tabletop:** This is the bent-knee variation, not straight-leg Reverse Plank. 
- **Gentle Seated Neck Side Bend:** A gentle warm-up drill, not a classical asana. 
- **Gentle Seated Neck Rotation:** A controlled mobility drill rather than a classical asana. 
- **Seated Eagle Arms:** Arm-only variation; this is not full standing Eagle pose. 
- **Cow Face Arms with Strap:** Arm-only variation on a chair; full Cow Face leg positioning is not included. 
- **Gentle Seated Wrist Circles:** A preparatory mobility drill, not a classical asana. Neutral hands with small wrist-position insets; the motion itself is not shown.
- **Gentle Wrist Extensor Stretch:** A preparatory forearm stretch, not a classical asana. 
- **Gentle Wrist Flexor Stretch:** A preparatory forearm stretch, not a classical asana. 
- **Seated Ankle Circles:** A seated joint-mobility drill rather than a classical asana. 
- **Supported Wall Calf Stretch:** A supported standing stretch used as yoga preparation, not a named classical asana. 
- **Supported Heel Raises:** A preparatory strength drill, not a classical asana. 
- **Single Knee-to-Chest:** Single-leg variation; Apanasana is also taught with both knees bent toward the trunk. 
- **Upright Bound Angle:** Active upright Bound Angle is kept distinct from the existing supported Yin Butterfly. 
- **Gentle Chair Seated Twist:** A chair-adapted twist is described without assigning a specific traditional seated-twist identity. 
- **Reclined Hamstring Stretch with Strap:** Supported variation; no need to hold the big toe directly. 
- **Staff Pose:** This is upright seated Staff, not a plank position sometimes given similar terminology. Seated on a folded blanket with palms supported on blocks.
- **High Crescent Lunge:** This is a rear-heel-lifted standing lunge. A specific traditional Sanskrit identity is not guessed. Arms-forward High Lunge, with rear knee off the mat.
