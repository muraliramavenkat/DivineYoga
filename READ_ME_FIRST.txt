DIVINEYOGA — SHARE EDITION 1.0

GET STARTED
1. Extract the entire DivineYoga-Share.zip file.
2. Open START_HERE.html in Chrome, Safari, Firefox or Edge.
3. Choose Open DivineYoga or Choose a daily routine.

Windows: right-click the ZIP > Extract All.
Mac: double-click the ZIP, then open the extracted folder.

No account, installation or internet connection is required. Keep the entire folder together; do not move only index.html or send one HTML file.

INCLUDED
46 illustrated guides: 38 postures/variations and 8 labelled warm-up drills.
Yin: 10 posture guides. Vinyasa: 11. Power: 10. Hatha: 13 plus 8 warm-up drills.
Nine body-area pages, each with at least 10 relevant posture guides.
Daily routine planner: four suggested practices, add/reorder/remove steps, adjustable timers, pause/skip and printing.
Original posture library: names, instructions, movement aims, modifications and precautions.

YOUR DAILY ROUTINE
Select a suggested practice, read the pose instructions and use a comfortable modification. Timings are optional suggestions. The timer does not automatically move you to the next position. Release slowly and rest before selecting Next. For movement drills, follow the repetitions in the guide rather than holding still.
Planner changes apply to the open page. Use Print routine to keep a copy.

SHARING
Send DivineYoga-Share.zip as a file or via a file-sharing link. The recipient should download and extract the entire package before opening START_HERE.html. No files are uploaded by the website and there is no account or tracking service.
Designed for Windows and Mac computers. Mobile handling of multi-file offline websites differs by device; this package is intended for computer use.

PRACTICE
The guide is educational, not an individual assessment. Stop for sharp pain, numbness, dizziness or breathlessness. Consult a qualified teacher for unfamiliar poses and obtain individual clinical advice where needed. Difficulty or body-area tags do not establish safety for pain, injury or pregnancy.
